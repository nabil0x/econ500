## Exam Pattern (MSc 501 — 70 marks, answer any 5 from 8)

| Topic | Likely Questions | Priority | Answer File |
|-------|-----------------|----------|-------------|
| **Consumer Theory** | 2–3 questions (preference axioms, duality, convexity proofs) | ⭐⭐⭐ | [`answers-consumer-theory.md`](./answers-consumer-theory.md) |
| **Production & Supply** | 2 questions (production sets, full CD analysis, competitive eq) | ⭐⭐⭐ | [`answers-demand-production.md`](./answers-demand-production.md) |
| **Welfare & Pareto** | 1–2 questions (CV/EV/CS, Hicks-Kaldor, welfare theorems) | ⭐⭐⭐ | [`answers-welfare.md`](./answers-welfare.md) |
| **General Equilibrium** | 1 question (Edgeworth box or existence/stability) | ⭐⭐ | [`answers-ge-game-extern-trade.md`](./answers-ge-game-extern-trade.md) |
| **Demand / Trade** | 0–1 question (elasticities or tariff — rotating topic) | ⭐ | [`answers-demand-production.md`](./answers-demand-production.md) / [`answers-ge-game-extern-trade.md`](./answers-ge-game-extern-trade.md) |

**Question types**: ~35% essay, ~25% proof, ~25% numerical, ~10% graphical, ~5% game theory

**See detailed analysis**: [`answers-cross-reference.md`](./answers-cross-reference.md) — Sections A (technique map), E (source overlap)

## Study Path (10-Day Plan)

### Phase 1 — Consumer Theory (Days 1–3)

| Day | Topic | Study | Practice |
|-----|-------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Preference axioms + convexity | [`basics.md`](./basics.md) → [`answers-consumer-theory.md`](./answers-consumer-theory.md) 1A.Q1–Q5 | Prove: SM→M→LNS |
| 2 | Utility fn, monotonicity, quasiconcavity | [`answers-consumer-theory.md`](./answers-consumer-theory.md) 1A.Q6–Q10 | Prove: dMRS/dX < 0 |
| 3 | Cobb-Douglas duality chain | [`answers-consumer-theory.md`](./answers-consumer-theory.md) 1B.Q1–Q5 | Full UMP→EMP→Roy's→Shephard's |

**See also**: [`resources/extracted_text/chapter-1-preference-and-choice.txt`](./resources/extracted_text/chapter-1-preference-and-choice.txt)

### Phase 2 — Production & Supply (Days 4–5.5)

| Day | Topic | Study | Practice |
|-----|-------|-------|----------|
| 4 | Production sets, EoS, cost/profit fns | [`answers-demand-production.md`](./answers-demand-production.md) 3A.Q1–Q5, 3B.Q1–Q2 | Draw production set properties |
| 5 | Full production analysis (CD) | [`answers-demand-production.md`](./answers-demand-production.md) 3A.Q6–Q8 | Solve (KL)^(1/3) SR→LR |
