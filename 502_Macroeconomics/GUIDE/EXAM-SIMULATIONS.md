### P1.Q1 — Solow: Steady State & Phase Diagram (14 marks) — 12 min
**(a)** Neoclassical assumptions + derive $\dot{k}=sf(k)-(n+g+\delta)k$. [4] **(b)** Solow phase diagram — why BGP stable? [4] **(c)** Permanent $\delta\uparrow$: $k,y,c$ transitions. [3] **(d)** Why "exogenous growth"? [3]
| Part | Key Points |
|------|-----------|
| **(a)** | CRS, Inada, $Y=K^\alpha(AL)^{1-\alpha}$. SS: $sf(k^*)=(n+g+\delta)k^*$. |
| **(b)** | $sf(k)$ concave × linear $(n+g+\delta)k$. $k<k^*\Rightarrow\dot{k}>0$, stable. |
| **(c)** | $\delta\uparrow$ shifts break-even up → lower $k^*$. $k,y$ fall gradually; $c$ overshoots. |
| **(d)** | $g$ exogenous. $s,n$ affect levels only. |
### P1.Q2 — Ramsey: Euler Equation & Phase Diagram (14 marks) — 12 min
Household $\max\int e^{-\rho t}u(c)dt$, $\dot{k}=f(k)-c-(n+g+\delta)k$, CRRA.
**(a)** Hamiltonian → Euler $\dot{c}/c=(f'(k)-\rho-\theta g)/\theta$. [5] **(b)** $\dot{k}=0$ and $\dot{c}=0$ loci, phase diagram, saddle path. [4] **(c)** Modified GR vs Solow GR: why $k^*_{R}<k^{gold}$? [3] **(d)** Role of transversality? [2]
| Part | Key Points |
|------|-----------|
| **(a)** | $H=u(c)+\lambda[f(k)-c-(n+g+\delta)k]$. FOC: $c^{-\theta}=\lambda$, $\dot{\lambda}=\lambda(\rho-f'+n+g+\delta)$. |
| **(b)** | $\dot{k}=0$: inverted-U $c=f(k)-(n+g+\delta)k$. $\dot{c}=0$: vertical $f'(k)=\rho+\theta g$. |
| **(c)** | Mod GR: $f'(k^*)=\rho+\theta g$. Solow GR: $f'(k^{gold})=n+g+\delta$. $\rho>n$ → $k_R^*<k^{gold}$. |
| **(d)** | TVC $\lim\lambda ke^{-\rho t}=0$ picks unique convergent saddle path. |
### P1.Q3 — Endogenous Growth: R&D BGP Dynamics (14 marks) — 12 min
$\dot{A}=B(a_L L)^\gamma A^\phi$, $n>0$. $Y=[(1-a_L)L]^{1-\alpha}K^\alpha$.
**(a)** Derive $g_A$, BGP $g_A^*$. [4] **(b)** $(g_A,g_K)$ space with both loci. [4] **(c)** $a_L\uparrow$: level vs growth effect? [3] **(d)** Compare $a_L$ role to $s$ in Solow. [3]
| Part | Key Points |
|------|-----------|
| **(a)** | $g_A=Ba_L^\gamma L^\gamma A^{\phi-1}$. BGP: $\gamma n+(\phi-1)g_A=0$ → $g_A^*=\gamma n/(1-\phi)$. |
| **(b)** | $\dot{g}_A=0$ horizontal at $g_A^*$; $\dot{g}_K=0$ = 45° line. BGP at intersection. |
| **(c)** | $a_L\uparrow$ → $\dot{A}$ jumps → $A$ level higher permanently. $g_A^*$ unchanged (level effect). |
| **(d)** | Both have level effects. R&D endogenises $g_A$ via $n,\phi$; Solow takes $g$ exogenous. |
### P1.Q4 — OLG: Two-Period Optimization (14 marks) — 12 min
Two periods: young ($w_t$), old ($c_{2t+1}=(1+r_{t+1})s_t$). CRRA, discount $\rho$.
**(a)** Budget + max → Euler linking $c_{1t},c_{2t+1}$. [5] **(b)** $s_t(w_t,r_{t+1})$; income vs substitution effects. [4] **(c)** $k_{t+1}(k_t)$ for CD $f(k)=k^\alpha$. [3] **(d)** OLG convergence vs Solow? [2]
| Part | Key Points |
|------|-----------|
| **(a)** | $c_{1t}+c_{2t+1}/(1+r_{t+1})=w_t$. Euler: $c_{2t+1}/c_{1t}=[(1+r_{t+1})/(1+\rho)]^{1/\theta}$. |
| **(b)** | $s_t=w_t\cdot\Psi(r)$. $r\uparrow$: substitution → save more; income → save less. Net depends on IES $=1/\theta$. |
| **(c)** | $k_{t+1}=s_t/(1+n)$. Log utility: $k_{t+1}=(1-\alpha)k_t^\alpha/[(2+\rho)(1+n)]$. |
| **(d)** | OLG $\lambda_{OLG}\approx 1-\alpha$; Solow $\lambda_S\approx(1-\alpha)(n+g+\delta)$. OLG faster. |
### P1.Q5 — R&D & Monopolistic Competition (14 marks) — 12 min
$Y=[\int_0^N L(i)^\phi di]^{1/\phi}$, $0<\phi<1$. $\dot{N}=BL_N$, $L=L_Y+L_N$.
**(a)** Demand for firm $i$ and markup. [4] **(b)** Monopoly profit $\pi(i)$ in terms of $L_Y$. [4] **(c)** PV of profits → equilibrium $L_N^*$. [3] **(d)** Why market under-provides R&D? [3]
| Part | Key Points |
|------|-----------|
| **(a)** | FOC: $p(i)=Y^{1-\phi}L(i)^{\phi-1}$. Markup $p=w/\phi$, factor $=1/\phi>1$. |
| **(b)** | $\pi=(p-w)L=w(1-\phi)/\phi\cdot L_Y/N$ (symmetric). |
| **(c)** | Free entry: $w=B\cdot V$, $V=\pi/r$ → $L_N^*=L-\phi r/[B(1-\phi)]$. |
| **(d)** | (1) Appropriability. (2) Intertemporal spillovers. (3) Consumer surplus not captured. $L_N<L_N^{social}$. |
### P1.Q6 — RBC: Household Labor Supply (14 marks) — 12 min
Household $\max E_0\sum\beta^t[\ln c_t+\eta\ln(1-l_t)]$. Firm $y_t=z_tk_t^\theta l_t^{1-\theta}$, $z_{t+1}=\phi z_t+\varepsilon_t$.
**(a)** Intratemporal labor-leisure condition. [3] **(b)** Euler eq. [3] **(c)** Prove $l$ wage-independent when $\phi=\beta$. [4] **(d)** Frisch elasticity and RBC importance. [4]
| Part | Key Points |
|------|-----------|
| **(a)** | $\eta c_t/(1-l_t)=w_t$: MRS = real wage. |
| **(b)** | $1/c_t=\beta E_t[(1+r_{t+1})/c_{t+1}]$ — consumption smoothing. |
| **(c)** | $\phi=\beta$: $c,w$ grow proportionally → $l_t$ constant. Income/substitution effects offset. |
| **(d)** | Frisch $=(1-l)/l$. Micro $\approx0.2-0.5$; RBC needs $>2$ → Hansen indivisible labor. |
### P1.Q7 — New Keynesian: IS-PC-Taylor (14 marks) — 12 min
Household $\dot{C}/C=\sigma(r_t-\rho)$. Calvo pricing. Output gap $x_t=y_t-y_t^n$.
**(a)** NK IS: $\dot{x}_t=\sigma(r_t-r_t^n)$. Wicksellian $r_t^n$? [4] **(b)** NK PC: $\dot{\pi}_t=\rho\pi_t-\kappa x_t$. Divine coincidence? [4] **(c)** Taylor rule: $\phi_\pi>1$ determinacy? [3] **(d)** Why $x,\pi$ both jump? Cost-push shock return? [3]
| Part | Key Points |
|------|-----------|
| **(a)** | Log-linearised Euler + market clearing → NK IS. $r^n=\rho+\sigma^{-1}g$. $r>r^n$ → $x\downarrow$. |
| **(b)** | Calvo → forward-looking PC. Divine coincidence: $x=0\Rightarrow\pi=0$. Only cost-push shocks break it. |
| **(c)** | $\phi_\pi>1$: real rate rises with $\pi$, stabilising. $\phi_\pi<1$ → inflation spirals. |
| **(d)** | Both forward-looking. Cost-push → $\pi\uparrow$ → CB raises $i$ → $x\downarrow$ → saddle path back. |
### P1.Q8 — Synthesis: Growth Models Compared (14 marks) — 12 min
**(a)** Sources of long-run growth: Solow vs Ramsey vs R&D. [4] **(b)** Saving exogenous in Solow, endogenous in Ramsey — transition effects. [4] **(c)** Conditions for fully endogenous growth? Knife-edge? [3] **(d)** "All growth models are exogenous in the very long run." Evaluate. [3]
| Part | Key Points |
|------|-----------|
| **(a)** | Solow: $g$ exogenous. Ramsey: same $g$, micro saving. R&D: $g_A$ depends on $n,\phi,\gamma,a_L$. |
| **(b)** | Solow $s$ fixed; Ramsey $s$ varies with $f'(k)$ vs $\rho+\theta g$. Ramsey $c$ jumps; Solow monotonic. |
| **(c)** | $\phi=1$ AND $n=0$. Knife-edge: $\phi$ must be exactly 1. |
| **(d)** | Semi-endog ($\phi<1$): $g_A^*=\gamma n/(1-\phi)$ — $n$ unexplained. Fully endog ($\phi=1,n=0$): knife-edge. Jones (1995): $\phi<1$ resolves. |

# Time Management Guide

## The 5-Minute Selection Strategy
