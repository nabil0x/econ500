## 2. Core Assumptions

| Assumption | Definition | Where Used | Key Implication | Explain |
|-----------|-----------|------------|-----------------|---------|
<a id="term-inada"></a>
| **Inada Conditions** | $\lim_{K\to0} F_K = \infty$, $\lim_{K\to\infty} F_K = 0$; same for $L$ | Solow, Ramsey, OLG | Guarantees interior steady state exists — economy neither collapses nor explodes | [→](#term-inada) |
<a id="term-crs-prod"></a>
| **CRS Production** | $F(\lambda K, \lambda AL) = \lambda F(K, AL)$ | All growth models | Allows analysis in per-effective-worker terms; factor payments exhaust output (Euler's theorem) | [Q1](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-01) |
<a id="term-diminishing-returns"></a>
| **Diminishing Marginal Returns** | $F_{KK} < 0$, $F_{LL} < 0$ | Solow, Ramsey | Each additional unit of capital adds less to output; makes steady state stable | [Q1](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-01) |
<a id="term-perfect-competition-macro"></a>
| **Perfect Competition** | Firms are price-takers in factor markets | Solow, Ramsey (baseline) | Factor prices equal marginal products: $r = f'(k) - \delta$, $w = f(k) - kf'(k)$ | [Q1](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-01) |
<a id="term-exogenous-saving"></a>
| **Exogenous Saving Rate** | $s$ is constant and exogenous | Solow | Simplifying assumption relaxed in Ramsey; $s$ becomes endogenous choice | [Q1](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-01) |
<a id="term-exogenous-tech"></a>
| **Exogenous Technological Progress** | $\dot{A}/A = g$, treated as given | Solow, Ramsey | Technology is "manna from heaven"; contrast with endogenous growth where $g_A$ is determined within the model | [Q1](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-01) |
<a id="term-exogenous-pop"></a>
| **Exogenous Population Growth** | $\dot{L}/L = n$, treated as given | Solow, Ramsey, R&D | Labour force grows at constant rate $n$; demographic transition changes $n$ over time | [Q2](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-02) |
<a id="term-crra-assumption"></a>
| **CRRA Utility** | $u(c) = (c^{1-\theta}-1)/(1-\theta)$ | Ramsey, RBC, NK | Constant IES $= 1/\theta$; $\theta > 0$; higher $\theta$ = more risk aversion / more consumption smoothing | [Q11](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-ramsey-05) |
<a id="term-no-ponzi"></a>
| **No-Ponzi Condition** | $\lim_{t\to\infty} a(t) e^{-\int_0^t (r(s)-n)ds} = 0$ | Ramsey, OLG | Prevents households from borrowing indefinitely; necessary for well-defined intertemporal problem | [Q18](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-03) |
<a id="term-non-rivalry"></a>
| **Non-Rivalry of Knowledge** | One agent's use of an idea does not reduce availability for others | Endogenous growth | Fundamental feature enabling increasing returns and sustained growth; distinguishes $A$ from $K$ and $L$ | [Q15](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-endogrowth-04) |
<a id="term-partial-excludability"></a>
| **Partial Excludability** | Difficult to fully prevent others from using an idea | Endogenous growth | Creates wedge between private and social returns; motivates patent policy and R&D subsidies | [Q15](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-endogrowth-04) |
<a id="term-monopolistic-competition"></a>
| **Monopolistic Competition** | Firms have market power over differentiated products | NK, R&D growth models | Required to generate profits that fund R&D; in NK, causes sticky-price frictions | [Q20](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-05) |
<a id="term-sticky-prices"></a>
| **Sticky Prices** | Firms cannot adjust prices instantly (menu costs, Calvo contracts) | NK DSGE | Creates short-run non-neutrality of monetary policy; generates NK Phillips curve | [../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md](./../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md) |
<a id="term-rational-expectations"></a>
| **Rational Expectations** | Agents use all available information; no systematic forecast errors | RBC, NK DSGE | Expectations are model-consistent; $E_t[\pi_{t+1}]$ appears in NK IS and PC equations | [../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md](./../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md) |

## 3. Key Theorems & Conditions

| Theorem | Statement | Where | Explain |
|---------|-----------|-------|---------|
<a id="term-golden-rule"></a>
| **Golden Rule** | $f'(k_{gold}) = n + g + \delta$; maximises steady-state consumption per effective worker $c^* = f(k^*) - (n+g+\delta)k^*$ | [Q4](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-04), [Q8](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-ramsey-02) | [→](#term-golden-rule) |
<a id="term-modified-golden-rule"></a>
| **Modified Golden Rule** | $f'(k^*) = \rho + \theta g + \delta$; steady-state condition in Ramsey model; $k^* < k_{gold}$ for $\rho > 0$ | [Q8](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-ramsey-02), [Q18](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-03) | [→](#term-modified-golden-rule) |
<a id="term-uzawa-theorem"></a>
| **Uzawa's Theorem** | Sustained steady-state growth requires purely labour-augmenting (Harrod-neutral) technological progress; $Y = F(K, AL)$ is the only form consistent with constant $K/Y$ | [Q21](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-01) | [→](#term-uzawa-theorem) |
<a id="term-euler-equation"></a>
| **Euler Equation** | $\dot{c}/c = (1/\theta)(f'(k) - \rho - \theta g - \delta)$; intertemporal consumption optimality condition | [Q11](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-ramsey-05), [Q23](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-03) | [→](#term-euler-equation) |
<a id="term-jones-critique"></a>
| **Jones Critique** | Fully endogenous models predict TFP growth rises with R&D labour; US data shows no such trend despite massive R&D increase — supports semi-endogenous ($\beta+\theta<1$) view | [Q14](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-endogrowth-03), [Q20](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-05) | [→](#term-jones-critique) |
<a id="term-conditional-convergence"></a>
| **Conditional Convergence** | Poor countries grow faster than rich ones controlling for $s$, $n$, $g$, $\delta$; $\lambda = (1-\alpha)(n+g+\delta)$ is model speed | [Q5](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-05), [Q17](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-02) | [→](#term-conditional-convergence) |
<a id="term-harrod-neutral"></a>
| **Harrod-Neutral (Labour-Augmenting) Tech Progress** | $Y = F(K, AL)$; technology multiplies labour effectiveness; only form consistent with steady-state growth (Uzawa) | [Q21](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-01) | [→](#term-harrod-neutral) |
<a id="term-hicks-neutral-macro"></a>
| **Hicks-Neutral (Output-Augmenting) Tech Progress** | $Y = A(t) F(K, L)$; technology multiplies output directly; $MP_K$ and $MP_L$ rise at same rate | [Q21](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-01) | [→](#term-hicks-neutral-macro) |
<a id="term-ricardian-equivalence"></a>
| **Ricardian Equivalence** | Under lump-sum taxes, debt-financed and tax-financed government spending have identical effects; private saving offsets government dissaving | [Q19](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-04) | [→](#term-ricardian-equivalence) |
<a id="term-chamley-judd"></a>
| **Chamley-Judd Result** | Optimal long-run capital income tax rate is zero in Ramsey model; distortion accumulates over time making capital taxation very inefficient | [Q19](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-04) | [→](#term-chamley-judd) |
<a id="term-scale-effect"></a>
| **Scale Effect** | Prediction that larger economies (more R&D workers) have faster TFP growth; rejected by Jones (1995); semi-endogenous models eliminate it | [Q14](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-endogrowth-03) | [→](#term-scale-effect) |
<a id="term-samuelson-condition-macro"></a>
| **Dynamic Efficiency** | Economy is dynamically efficient if $k \le k_{gold}$ (consumption not reducible by lowering saving); Solow can have $k > k_{gold}$ (oversaving), Ramsey always has $k^* < k_{gold}$ | [Q4](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-04), [Q8](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-ramsey-02) | [→](#term-samuelson-condition-macro) |
<a id="term-growth-accounting"></a>
| **Growth Accounting** | $\dot{Y}/Y = \dot{A}/A + \alpha \dot{K}/K + (1-\alpha)\dot{L}/L$; decomposes growth into TFP (Solow residual) and factor accumulation | [Q17](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-02) | [→](#term-growth-accounting) |

## 4. Key Models

| Model | Form / Core Equation | Key Properties | Appears In | Explain |
|-------|------|---------------|------------|---------|
<a id="term-solow"></a>
| **Solow Growth Model** | $\dot{k} = s f(k) - (n+g+\delta)k$ | CRS, diminishing returns, Inada conditions; exogenous $s$, $n$, $g$; unique stable steady state; level effects only from policy | [Q1–Q6](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-01), [Q16](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-01), [Q21](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-01) | — |
<a id="term-ramsey"></a>
| **Ramsey-Cass-Koopmans** | $\dot{k} = f(k) - c - (n+g+\delta)k$, $\dot{c}/c = (f'(k) - \rho - \theta g - \delta)/\theta$ | Endogenous saving; saddle-path stability; $k^* < k_{gold}$; slower convergence than Solow | [Q7–Q11](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-ramsey-01), [Q18](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-03), [Q23](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-03) | — |
<a id="term-endogenous-growth"></a>
| **R&D Endogenous Growth (Romer/GH/AGH)** | $\dot{A} = B (a^K K)^\beta (a^L L)^\theta A^\phi$ | Knowledge endogenously produced; non-rivalry $\to$ increasing returns; monopolistic competition; semi-endogenous ($\beta+\theta<1$) or fully endogenous ($\beta+\theta=1$) | [Q12–Q15](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-endogrowth-01), [Q20](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-05), [Q22](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-02), [Q24](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-04) | — |
<a id="term-olgi-model"></a>
| **Overlapping Generations (OLG)** | $k_{t+1} = \frac{1}{(1+n)(1+g)} s(k_t)$ | Discrete time; two-period lives; can generate dynamic inefficiency ($k > k_{gold}$); speed of convergence differs from Solow | [../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md](./../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md) | — |
<a id="term-rbc-model"></a>
| **Real Business Cycle (RBC)** | $y_t = z_t k_t^\theta l_t^{1-\theta}$, $z_{t+1} = \phi z_t + \varepsilon_t$ | Technology shocks drive cycles; flexible prices; log utility; Hansen indivisible labour solves labour-supply volatility puzzle | [../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md](./../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md) | — |
<a id="term-nk-dsge"></a>
| **New Keynesian DSGE** | NK IS: $x_t = E_t x_{t+1} - \sigma^{-1}(i_t - E_t\pi_{t+1} - r_t^n)$; NK PC: $\pi_t = \beta E_t\pi_{t+1} + \kappa x_t$ | Sticky prices $\to$ monetary non-neutrality; divine coincidence when only demand shocks; Taylor rule closes model | [../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md](./../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md), [../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md](./../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md) | — |

## 5. Equilibrium & Growth Concepts

| Concept | Definition | See | Explain |
|---------|-----------|-----|---------|
<a id="term-steady-state"></a>
| **Steady State** | $\dot{k} = 0$; $sf(k^*) = (n+g+\delta)k^*$; economy stops changing in per-effective-worker terms | [Q1](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-01) | [→](#term-steady-state) |
<a id="term-saddle-path"></a>
| **Saddle Path** | Unique convergent trajectory in Ramsey phase diagram; consumption jumps to this path; slope positive in $(k,c)$ space near steady state | [Q7](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-ramsey-01), [Q23](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-03) | [→](#term-saddle-path) |
<a id="term-phase-diagram"></a>
| **Phase Diagram** | $(k, c)$ space with $\dot{k}=0$ (hump-shaped) and $\dot{c}=0$ (vertical) loci; four directional quadrants; unique saddle path to $(k^*, c^*)$ | [Q7](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-ramsey-01) | [→](#term-phase-diagram) |
<a id="term-transition-dynamics"></a>
| **Transition Dynamics** | Adjustment path from initial $k(0)$ to $k^*$; Solow: monotonic; Ramsey: $c$ jumps then both converge along saddle path | [Q3](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-03), [Q7](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-ramsey-01) | [→](#term-transition-dynamics) |
<a id="term-speed-of-convergence"></a>
| **Speed of Convergence** | $\lambda_{solow} = (1-\alpha)(n+g+\delta)$; $\lambda_{ramsey} \approx 0.04$; Solow converges faster because fixed $s$ does not smooth consumption | [Q9](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-ramsey-03), [Q17](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-02) | [→](#term-speed-of-convergence) |
<a id="term-dynamic-inefficiency"></a>
| **Dynamic Inefficiency** | $k > k_{gold}$; saving rate too high; reducing $s$ would increase steady-state consumption permanently; possible in Solow and OLG, not in Ramsey | [Q4](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-solow-04), [OLG](./../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md) | [→](#term-dynamic-inefficiency) |
<a id="term-semi-endogenous"></a>
| **Semi-Endogenous Growth** | $\beta + \theta < 1$; steady-state $g_A^* = ((\theta+\beta)n)/(1-\phi-\beta)$; policy has level effects only; long-run growth driven solely by population growth $n$ | [Q12](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-endogrowth-01), [Q14](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-endogrowth-03) | [→](#term-semi-endogenous) |
<a id="term-fully-endogenous"></a>
| **Fully Endogenous Growth** | $\beta + \theta = 1$, $n = 0$; policy can permanently raise growth rate; scale effect predicts larger R&D workforce $\to$ faster TFP growth (rejected by data) | [Q14](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-endogrowth-03), [Q20](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-05) | [→](#term-fully-endogenous) |
<a id="term-poverty-trap"></a>
| **Poverty Trap** | Self-reinforcing mechanism keeping economy at low income; $\dot{k}/k$ crosses zero at three points — two stable steady states with unstable threshold in between | [Q25](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-05) | [→](#term-poverty-trap) |
<a id="term-convergence-club"></a>
| **Convergence Club** | Group of countries converging to same steady state; multiple clubs exist due to threshold externalities, complementarities, institutional traps | [Q25](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-05) | [→](#term-convergence-club) |
<a id="term-big-push"></a>
| **Big Push** | Coordinated investment across multiple sectors to escape poverty trap; policy implication of multiple equilibria (Rosenstein-Rodan, Sachs) | [Q25](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-05) | [→](#term-big-push) |
<a id="term-level-effect"></a>
| **Level Effect vs Growth Effect** | Level effect: permanent change in $k^*, y^*$ but not growth rate; Growth effect: permanent change in long-run $\dot{y}/y$; Solow: level only; Endogenous: possible growth effect | [Q16](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-01), [Q20](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch47-macro-05) | [→](#term-level-effect) |
<a id="term-taylor-rule"></a>
| **Taylor Rule** | $i_t = \bar{r} + \pi_t + \phi_\pi(\pi_t - \bar{\pi}) + \phi_x x_t$; central bank adjusts nominal rate in response to inflation and output gap; $\phi_\pi > 1$ ensures determinacy | [../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md](./../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md) | [→](#term-taylor-rule) |
<a id="term-wicksellian-rate"></a>
| **Wicksellian (Natural) Rate** | $r_t^n = \rho + \sigma^{-1} g$; real interest rate that would prevail with flexible prices; benchmark for monetary policy in NK model | [../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md](./../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md) | [→](#term-wicksellian-rate) |
<a id="term-learning-by-doing"></a>
| **Learning by Doing** | $\dot{A} = \delta L$; knowledge accumulates as byproduct of production experience (Arrow, 1962); form of endogenous growth without formal R&D | [Q15](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-endogrowth-04), [Q24](../ANSWERS/answers-macroeconomics.md#q-batch48-macro-04) | [→](#term-learning-by-doing) |
