## Exam Pattern (MSc 502 — 70 marks, answer any 5 from 8)

| # | Topic | Marks | Appears In | Answer Status |
|---|-------|-------|------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Solow Growth Model** | 14 | Batch 47 Q1–Q2, Batch 48 Q1 | ✅ Answered (Q1–Q6, Q16–Q17, Q21) |
| 2 | **Ramsey-Cass-Koopmans** | 14 | Batch 47 Q3, Batch 48 Q3 | ✅ Answered (Q7–Q11, Q18–Q19, Q23) |
| 3 | **Endogenous Growth & R&D (Romer/GH/AGH)** | 14 | Batch 47 Q5, Batch 48 Q2, Q5 | ✅ Answered (Q12–Q15, Q20, Q22, Q24) |
| 4 | **Overlapping Generations (OLG)** | 14 | Batch 47 Q4, Batch 48 Q4 | ❌ Missing from answers |
| 5 | **R&D & Monopolistic Competition** | 14 | Batch 47 Q6, Batch 48 Q6 | ❌ Missing from answers |
| 6 | **Real Business Cycle (RBC)** | 14 | Batch 47 Q7, Batch 48 Q7 | ❌ Missing from answers |
| 7 | **New Keynesian DSGE** | 14 | Batch 47 Q8, Batch 48 Q8 | ❌ Missing from answers |
| 8 | **Rotating / Synthesis** | 14 | Batch 47 (any), Batch 48 (any) | Varies |

**Question structure:** Each question is 14 marks with 3–4 sub-parts (consecutive). Mix of derivation (~35%), phase diagram/graphical (~25%), numerical (~20%), essay (~20%).

**Instructions:** Answer any **five** questions. All parts consecutively. Time: 4 Hours.

## Study Path (10-Day Plan)

### Phase 1 — Solow Growth Model (Days 1–3)

| Day | Topic | Study | Practice |
|-----|-------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Neoclassical assumptions, k-dot derivation, steady state | [`answers-macroeconomics.md`](./answers-macroeconomics.md) Q1, Q6 | Derive $\dot{k}=sf(k)-(n+g+\delta)k$ from scratch |
| 2 | Golden Rule, saving rate effects, level vs growth effects | Q4, Q16d, Q17a | Prove $s_{gold}=\alpha$, compute $k^*$ elasticity |
| 3 | Transition dynamics, convergence, comparative statics, growth accounting | Q2, Q3, Q5, Q17b–d, Q21 | Draw Solow diagram for $\delta\downarrow$ and $n\downarrow$ |

**See also:** [`../../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md`](../question-banks/../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md) Q1–Q2, [`../../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md`](../question-banks/../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md) Q1

### Phase 2 — Ramsey-Cass-Koopmans (Days 3–5)

| Day | Topic | Study | Practice |
|-----|-------|-------|----------|
| 3 | Household problem, Euler equation derivation | Q7, Q11, Q18a | Set up Hamiltonian, derive $\dot{c}/c$ |
| 4 | Phase diagram ($\dot{k}=0$, $\dot{c}=0$ loci), saddle path, steady state | Q7, Q18c, Q23b–d | Draw full phase diagram, label 4 quadrants |
| 5 | Speed of adjustment, government spending shocks, welfare | Q9, Q10, Q19 | Taylor linearization, compare Solow vs Ramsey $\lambda$ |

**See also:** [`../../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md`](../question-banks/../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md) Q3, [`../../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md`](../question-banks/../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md) Q3

### Phase 3 — Endogenous Growth & R&D (Days 5–7)

| Day | Topic | Study | Practice |
|-----|-------|-------|----------|
| 5 | R&D knowledge accumulation, role of $a^L$, level vs growth effects | Q12, Q15 | Derive $g_A^* = \gamma n/(1-\phi)$ |
| 6 | $(\beta+\theta)$ cases: semi-endogenous vs fully endogenous | Q13, Q14, Q20 | Draw $(g_A, g_K)$ space with both loci |
| 7 | Impact vs steady-state effects of $L_R$ increase, nature of knowledge | Q22, Q24, Q25 | Calculate $\Delta\ln A$ from 10% $L_R$ increase |

**See also:** [`../../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md`](../question-banks/../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-47/macro-502.md) Q5, [`../../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md`](../question-banks/../../question-banks/batch-finals/batch-48/macro-502.md) Q2, Q5

## Exam Day Checklist — Most Repeated Questions

These topics appear in **both Batch 47 and Batch 48** (highest probability):

### Solow (2+ appearances)

- [ ] Neoclassical assumptions + derive $\dot{k} = sf(k) - (n+g+\delta)k$ → [`answers-macroeconomics.md`](./answers-macroeconomics.md) Q1, Q16
- [ ] Phase diagram for $k$ and Balanced Growth Path → Q1, Q16, Q21
- [ ] Effects of changes in $\delta$, $g$, $\alpha$ on break-even and actual investment → Q2, Q16c, Q21c
- [ ] Saving ratio: level effects, not growth effects (proof) → Q4, Q6, Q16d, Q17
- [ ] Golden Rule of capital accumulation → Q4, Q17a
- [ ] Speed of convergence $\lambda = (1-\alpha)(n+g+\delta)$ → Q6, Q17b

### Ramsey (2+ appearances)

- [ ] Euler equation derivation via Hamiltonian → Q7, Q11, Q18a, Q23
- [ ] Phase diagram: $\dot{k}=0$ and $\dot{c}=0$ loci, saddle path → Q7, Q18, Q23
- [ ] Socially optimal (Modified Golden Rule) vs Golden Rule → Q8, Q18c, Q23c
- [ ] Speed of adjustment (Taylor approximation) → Q9, Q23d
- [ ] Government spending shock (permanent vs temporary) → Q10, Q19

### Endogenous Growth & R&D (2+ appearances)

- [ ] Role of $a^L$ in knowledge accumulation (level vs growth effect) → Q12, Q20b, Q24a
- [ ] $\dot{g}_A=0$ and $\dot{g}_K=0$ loci in $(g_A, g_K)$ space → Q13, Q20c, Q24b
- [ ] Semi-endogenous ($\beta+\theta<1$) vs fully endogenous ($\beta+\theta=1$, $n=0$) → Q14, Q20d, Q24c
- [ ] Nature of knowledge: non-rivalry, partial excludability, cumulativeness → Q15, Q24d
- [ ] Impact vs steady-state effect of $L_R$ increase → Q22

### OLG, R&D Monopolistic Competition, RBC, New Keynesian (Missing — study from batch finals)

- [ ] OLG: two-period maximization, $k_{t+1}(k_t)$ dynamics, convergence speed → Batch 47 Q4, Batch 48 Q4
- [ ] R&D Monopolistic Competition: patent demand, equilibrium $L_A$, social optimum → Batch 47 Q6, Batch 48 Q6
- [ ] RBC: household problem, labor supply, Hansen indivisible labor → Batch 47 Q7, Batch 48 Q7
- [ ] New Keynesian: Lucas supply, NK IS, NK PC, divine coincidence, Taylor rule → Batch 47 Q8, Batch 48 Q8
