## What Is This Topic About?

Why Venezuela — once the richest country in South America — collapsed into hyperinflation, poverty, and mass migration. And what other developing countries (including Bangladesh) should learn from it.

---

## Key Definitions

| Term | Simple Explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| **Oil dependency** | When a country relies on oil for most of its export earnings (~95% for Venezuela) |
| **Dutch disease** | When a resource boom makes other sectors uncompetitive (currency appreciates, manufacturing dies) |
| **Hyperinflation** | Extremely rapid inflation that destroys the value of money (prices double every few weeks) |
| **Diversification** | Having many different industries/sectors so if one fails, others survive |
| **Rentier state** | A country that gets most of its revenue from external resources (like oil) rather than taxing its own citizens |
| **Institutional strength** | How well a country's systems work (courts, central bank, civil service) — independently and reliably |

## The Big Picture — What Happened to Venezuela

```
Timeline:
1970s-80s: Oil boom → high spending, borrowing
1990s:  Oil prices fell → crisis → political instability
2000s:  Oil boom again → "socialism of the 21st century"
2014:   Oil prices crashed → everything unraveled
Present: Hyperinflation, 6M+ refugees, economic collapse
```

---

## Why Venezuela Matters for Development Economics

| Lesson | Why It Matters |
|--------|---------------|
| **Diversification** | If you rely on one export, a price drop destroys everything |
| **Institutions matter** | Independent central bank, rule of law, anti-corruption — these prevent collapse |
| **Populism is risky** | Short-term giveaways without building productive capacity create vulnerability |
| **Macro discipline** | Printing money to cover deficits destroys savings and wages |

---

## Simple Analogy

> Venezuela is like a person who earns all their money from one freelance client. When the client stops paying, they have no backup plan. They also spent all their savings during the good times. Then they start printing fake money to pay bills, which makes everything worthless.

## Connection to Bangladesh

| Venezuela | Bangladesh |
|-----------|-----------|
| Oil = 95% of exports | RMG = 86% of exports |
| Didn't diversify | Still not diversified enough |
| Weak institutions | Governance challenges |
| Political instability | Political challenges |

> **Exam point:** Bangladesh shares Venezuela's vulnerability — over-concentration in one export sector.
