## 1. Seven Structural Concerns

### Concern 1: Declining Trade Orientation
| Metric | 2012 | 2022 |
|--------|------|------|
| Trade-to-GDP ratio | 48.1% | **33.8%** |
| Comparison | — | Vietnam: >150%; India: higher |

### Concern 2: Export Concentration (RMG Dominance)
| Metric | 2010s | 2023 |
|--------|-------|------|
| RMG share in exports | 77% | **86%** |
| Export concentration index | — | >2x India/Indonesia/Vietnam |

### Concern 3: Capital Flight via Trade Mis-invoicing
- Annual average loss: **~$8.27 billion** (2009–2018)
- Method: Over and under-invoicing of trade
- IMF noted financial account outflows of 0.5% of GDP (FY2022–23)

### Concern 4: Export Data Mismatch
| Period | EPB Reported | Bangladesh Bank | Gap |
|--------|-------------|----------------|-----|
| 2020–21 | — | — | **$4.73B** |
| 2022–23 | — | — | **$12B** |
| 2023–24 (Jul-Apr) | $47.47B | $33.67B | **$13.8B** |

### Concern 5: RMG Sector Challenges
- Employs 4+ million workers (60% women)
- LDC graduation 2026 = loss of GSP preferences
- Need to diversify into non-cotton/man-made fibre (MMF)

### Concern 6: High Tariff Regime
| Entity | Average Tariff |
|--------|---------------|
| Bangladesh | **27%** |
| Low-income countries avg | 9.79% |
| Global average | 6% |

> Creates **anti-export bias** — firms prefer selling domestically

### Concern 7: Limited FTA Participation
- Only bilateral FTA: **Bhutan (2020)** — narrow scope
- SAFTA: hindered by non-tariff barriers
- BIMSTEC FTA: delayed
- Vietnam has CPTPP + EU-Vietnam FTA

---

## 2. Policy Recommendations

| Area | Recommendation |
|------|---------------|
| **Trade diversification** | Reduce tariffs on capital goods/intermediate inputs; tax incentives for non-RMG sectors |
| **RMG sustainability** | Diversify into MMF textiles; green finance fund; enforce labour standards |
| **Tariff reform** | Remove anti-export bias through strategic liberalization |
| **Data accuracy** | Digital tracking systems; cross-verification EPB/Bangladesh Bank |
| **FTA strategy** | Join RCEP; expedite BIMSTEC; negotiate strategic FTAs |
