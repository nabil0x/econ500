## What Is This Topic About?

How Bangladesh trades with the world — what it exports, what it imports, and the structural problems in its trade regime that make the economy vulnerable.

---

## Key Definitions

| Term | Simple Explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| **Trade-to-GDP ratio** | Total trade (exports + imports) as % of GDP — measures how open an economy is |
| **Export concentration** | When a country depends heavily on ONE product for most export earnings |
| **Comparative advantage** | A country should specialize in what it produces relatively most efficiently |
| **Tariff** | A tax on imported goods — makes imports more expensive |
| **Anti-export bias** | When trade policies make it more profitable to sell domestically than to export |
| **Trade mis-invoicing** | Falsifying export/import values to move money out of the country illegally |
| **FTA (Free Trade Agreement)** | Agreement between countries to reduce/eliminate tariffs on trade |
| **GSP (Generalized System of Preferences)** | Preferential tariff treatment for developing countries |
| **LDC graduation** | When a country is no longer classified as Least Developed Country — loses trade preferences |

## Bangladesh Trade Structure — The Big Problem

```
EXPORTS:                          IMPORTS:
┌─────────────────────┐          ┌─────────────────────┐
│  RMG (86%)          │          │  Raw materials      │
│                     │          │  Machinery          │
│  Leather (small)    │          │  Food               │
│  Jute (small)       │          │  Fuel               │
│  Others (tiny)      │          │  Consumer goods     │
└─────────────────────┘          └─────────────────────┘

Very concentrated                    Very diverse
(one product rules)                  (no single dependency)
```

**Problem:** If RMG exports fall, Bangladesh has NO other major export to fall back on.

---

## Why Tariffs Matter

| Who | Average Tariff | Effect |
|-----|---------------|--------|
| Bangladesh | **27%** | Makes imports expensive |
| Global average | 6% | Bangladesh is an outlier |
| Vietnam | Much lower | They're more competitive |

**The paradox:** High tariffs on imported inputs (like fabric, machinery) make it harder for Bangladeshi exporters to compete globally.

---

## Why FTAs Matter

| Country | FTAs Signed | Advantage |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| **Bangladesh** | 1 (Bhutan) | Limited market access |
| **Vietnam** | CPTPP, EU FTA, many others | Preferential access to major markets |

---

## Simple Analogy

> Bangladesh's trade is like a shop that sells 86% of its revenue from one product. It buys inputs at high prices (high tariffs), has few special deals (FTAs), and some money leaks out (mis-invoicing). When competitors get better deals from customers, the shop struggles.
