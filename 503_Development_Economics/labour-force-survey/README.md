## 1. Core Definitions

| Term | Definition | Bangladesh 2022 |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Working-Age Population** | Aged 15+ | **119.37 million** |
| **Labour Force** | Employed + unemployed seeking work | **73.05 million** |
| **Employed** | Worked ≥1 hour for pay/profit in reference week | **70.47 million** |
| **Unemployed** | No work, available, actively seeking | **2.58 million** |
| **Underemployed** | Working <35 hrs/week, willing to work more | **2.82 million (4.01%)** |

---

## 2. Key Rates

| Indicator | Overall | Male | Female |
|-----------|---------|------|--------|
| **LFPR** | **61.2%** | 80% | 42.77% |
| **Unemployment Rate** | **3.53%** | 3.50% | 3.59% |

---

## 3. Employment by Sector

| Sector | Share | Rural | Urban |
|--------|-------|-------|-------|
| **Agriculture** | **45.4%** | 57.4% | 9.3% |
| **Industry** | **17.0%** | 13.5% | 27.5% |
| **Services** | **37.6%** | 29.1% | 63.2% |

> Key point: Agriculture still largest employer despite declining share; Services dominate urban areas

---

## 4. Employment by Status

| Status | Share |
|--------|-------|
| Own-account workers | **50.6%** |
| Employees | 38.1% |
| Contributing family workers | 6.8% |

> **Implication:** High informal/self-employment — limited formal sector absorption

---

## 5. Trends (2010–2022)

| Metric | 2010 | 2022 | Change |
|--------|------|------|--------|
| Total employed | 54.1M | **70.47M** | +30% |
| Male employed | 37.9M | 45.61M | +20% |
| Female employed | 16.2M | **24.86M** | **+53%** |

> Female employment grew much faster — linked to RMG expansion and other sectors

---

## 6. Youth Labour Force (15–29)

- Youth labour force: **26.82 million**
- Youth unemployment: **Higher than national average**
- Educated youth more affected by unemployment
- NEET (Not in Education, Employment, or Training): Higher among females
