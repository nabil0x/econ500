### A1 — Bangladesh as Positive Outlier
**How do you explain Bangladesh's status as a 'positive outlier' in cross-country comparisons of social progress compared to countries with similar income levels?**
- *Key concepts:* Bangladesh paradox, Kabeer's thesis, women's renegotiation of patriarchy, Sen's two pathways, comparative evidence (India, Pakistan)

### A2 — Classical Patriarchy & Women's Agency
**(i) What is Classical Patriarchy?**
**(ii) What are the different forms of women's agency?**
**(iii) Explain the evolution of women's agency in Bangladesh's development trajectory.**
- *Key concepts:* Kandiyoti's classical patriarchy, patrilineal/patrilocal systems, adaptive preferences, hidden transcripts, bargaining, patriarchal bargains, four phases of agency evolution in Bangladesh
- *Also appears in:* IMG OCR (2nd Tutorial Q2)
- **→ [Answer Q2](./answers/02-classical-patriarchy-agency.md) | 📖 [Kabeer notes](./naila-kabeer/README.md) | 📄 [Ch.1 PDF](./Naila%20kabeer/Naila%20Kabeer%20chapter%201%20Unravelling%20the%20paradox.pdf)**

### A3 — Labour Force & Underutilization
**(A) What is meant by 'Persons in Employment' and 'Labor Underutilization'? Use UN definitions.**
**(B) Are persons in own-use production work (e.g., subsistence farming, unpaid domestic work) considered 'employed'? Justify.**
**(C) What is the proportion of unemployed youth (aged 15-29) in total unemployment? Explain the NEET situation.**
- *Key concepts:* 19th ICLS definitions, LU1-LU4 framework, own-use production work boundary, one-hour criterion, youth unemployment, NEET
- *Also appears in:* IMG OCR (2nd Tutorial Q3)
- **→ [Answer Q3](./answers/03-labour-force-underutilization.md) | 📖 [LFS notes](./labour-force-survey/README.md) | 📄 [LFS report](./LFS/LFS%202022.pdf)**

### A4 — Venezuela Crisis
**From Venezuela's experience as an 'Oil Rich Country', explain their investment and governance structure. How does it explain their current economic and social crisis?**
- *Key concepts:* Oil dependency (~95% exports), Dutch disease, rentier state, central bank capture, hyperinflation, resource curse, Bangladesh comparison (RMG 86%)
- *Cross-reference:* Venezuela question (IMG-20260411-WA0001.jpg)

## Section B — 3rd Tutorial (MainQ.pdf, Page 9)

> **Topics:** Bangladesh macroeconomy, external trade challenges, Iran oil strategy

### B1 — Bangladesh Macro Overview
**If you were to present Bangladesh's macro-economic scenario to a foreign audience, which indicators would you use and why? How would you present a brief macro-economic overview? (5 + 15 marks)**
- *Key concepts:* 6 indicator clusters (growth, employment, trade, social, fiscal, monetary), incomplete structural transformation, external vulnerability, Bangladesh paradox, fiscal constraints
- **→ [Answer Q5](./answers/05-bangladesh-macro-overview.md) | 📖 [Trade notes](./bangladesh-trade/README.md)**

### B2 — External Trade Challenges
**Briefly unveil the key challenges of external trade in Bangladesh amidst the current dynamic landscape.**
- *Key concepts:* 7 structural concerns — declining trade openness (33.8%), RMG concentration (86%), capital flight ($8.27B/yr), EPB-BB data mismatch ($13.8B), RMG sustainability, high tariffs (27%), limited FTAs (1 — Bhutan)
- **→ [Answer Q6](./answers/06-external-trade-challenges.md) | 📄 [Trade DOCX](./2nd%20tuto/External%20Trade_BD.docx)**

### B3 — Iran Oil Strategy
**How did Iran strategize its oil dependency and use of energy resources over decades amidst global challenges? How did the crises demonstrate pressure on economic and social outcomes? (10 + 10 marks)**
- *Key concepts:* Phases of Iran's oil strategy (1950s-present), massive energy subsidisation, sanctions impact, currency crisis, inflation >40%, comparison with Venezuela
- *Also appears in:* Iran Ppt (1).pptx (reference)
- **→ [Answer Q7](./answers/07-iran-oil-strategy.md) | 📄 [Iran PPTX](./Iran%20Ppt%20(1).pptx)**
