## 🎯 Exam Question

> *"Venezuela is not just an economic collapse, it is a lesson on governance and diversification. Write a comprehensive explanation."* (< 1000 words)

---

## Core Argument

Venezuela's collapse was **not inevitable** — it was the result of specific policy failures and structural vulnerabilities that offer lessons for all developing countries.

---

## Key Factors in the Collapse

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Oil dependency** | ~95% of export earnings from oil; virtually no diversification |
| **Price shock** | 2014 oil price crash exposed the fragility of the entire economy |
| **Mismanagement** | Price controls, expropriations, currency manipulation |
| **Hyperinflation** | One of the worst in modern history — destroyed savings and wages |
| **Production collapse** | Oil output fell from 3.2M bpd → <500k bpd |
| **Humanitarian crisis** | Mass migration (6M+ refugees), food/medicine shortages |

---

## The Diversification Lesson

```
Single Export Economy
        │
        ├── Vulnerable to price shocks
        ├── No buffer sectors when prices fall
        ├── Foreign exchange dries up → can't import essentials
        └── Entire economy rises and falls with one commodity
```

**Contrast with diversified economies:** Multiple sectors provide stability; if one falls, others compensate.

---

## The Governance Lesson

| Failure | Consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| Eroded institutional independence | Central bank printed money → hyperinflation |
| Weak rule of law | Expropriation → investment collapse |
| Corruption | Oil revenue misappropriated |
| No checks and balances | Rapid policy swings based on political whims |
| Destroyed state capacity | Cannot deliver basic services |

---

## Application to Bangladesh

| Bangladesh Vulnerability | Venezuela Parallel |
|------------------------|-------------------|
| RMG = 86% of exports | Oil = 95% of exports |
| Remittance dependence | — |
| Tariff-heavy trade regime | Closed economy policies |
| Governance challenges | Institutional weakness |

> **Key exam point:** Bangladesh should learn from Venezuela — diversify exports, strengthen institutions, maintain macroeconomic discipline.

- Inflation peaked at over 1,000,000% (hyperinflation)
- 6M+ Venezuelans fled the country
