## What Is This Topic About?

The idea that poor countries are NOT "behind" — they've been deliberately **kept poor** by rich countries. Underdevelopment is the flip side of development: the West got rich by making the rest poor.

---

## Key Definitions

| Term | Simple Explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| **Centre (Metropolis)** | Rich, industrialized countries that control technology, capital, and markets |
| **Periphery (Satellite)** | Poor countries that provide raw materials, cheap labor, and markets for the centre |
| **Unequal exchange** | Periphery sells raw materials cheap, buys manufactured goods expensive |
| **Terms of trade** | The ratio of export prices to import prices — worsens for periphery over time |
| **Neocolonialism** | Former colonies are politically independent but still economically controlled |
| **Underdevelopment** | Not a natural state — it's actively CREATED by the global capitalist system |

---

## The Core Idea — One Sentence

> *"The development of the West came at the cost of the underdevelopment of the rest."*

## How Dependency Differs from Modernization Theory

| Aspect | Modernization Theory | Dependency Theory |
|--------|---------------------|-------------------|
| **Why are countries poor?** | They are "backward" and traditional | They were actively underdeveloped by colonialism |
| **What should they do?** | Copy the West | Break free from exploitative relationships |
| **Is the global system fair?** | Yes — trade benefits everyone | No — trade enriches the centre at the expense of the periphery |
| **Can all countries develop?** | Yes — through the 5 stages | No — the system is designed to keep the periphery down |

---

## Simple Analogy

> A landlord and tenant: the landlord got rich by charging the tenant high rent. Over time, the tenant gets poorer while the landlord gets richer. Modernization theory says "the tenant should work harder and save more." Dependency theory says "the tenant is poor BECAUSE of the landlord."
