## What Is This Topic About?

The conflict between growing crops to export (cash crops) vs growing food to feed your own people (food crops). It's really a conflict between **dependency** (relying on global markets) and **autonomy** (controlling your own food supply).

---

## Key Definitions

| Term | Simple Explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| **Cash crop** | A crop grown to sell on international markets (cotton, coffee, cocoa, peanuts, flowers) |
| **Food crop** | A crop grown to feed local people (rice, wheat, maize, millet) |
| **Terms of trade** | The ratio of export prices to import prices — worsens for commodity exporters over time |
| **Comprador bourgeoisie** | A local business class that profits from being the middleman between foreign companies and the local economy |
| **Unequal exchange** | The gap between what the periphery sells (cheap raw materials) and what it buys (expensive manufactured goods) |
| **Dependency** | When a country's economy depends on forces outside its control |

## The Core Conflict

```
Cash Crop Model                      Food Crop Model
(Export-led growth)                  (Self-sufficiency)
        │                                    │
  Grow coffee/flowers/              Grow rice/maize/
  cotton for export                 vegetables for local
        │                                    │
  Earn foreign exchange             Feed your own people
        │                                    │
  But vulnerable to:                 But limited foreign
  • price crashes                    exchange earnings
  • buyer decisions
  • climate shocks
```

## How Colonialism Created This Problem

| Before Colonialism | After Colonialism |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Africa grew food for itself | Africa grows cash crops for export |
| Diverse farming, food secure | Specialized in 1-2 crops, food insecure |
| Local control over land | Land taken for plantations |
| Local skills and knowledge | Traditional knowledge destroyed |

> **Key point:** Pre-colonial Africa was food self-sufficient. Hunger in Africa is NOT natural — it was created.

## The Numbers that Shocked the World

| Year | Bananas for 1 Tractor |
|------|----------------------|
| 1960 | 3 tons |
| 1970 | **11 tons** — you need 4x more bananas for the same tractor |

| Year | 25 tons Rubber = |
|------|-----------------|
| 1960 | 6 tractors |
| 1975 | **2 tractors** |

> This is "deteriorating terms of trade" — cash crop producers get less and less for what they sell, while paying more and more for what they buy.

---

## Simple Analogy

> A farmer is told to stop growing food for their family and instead grow roses for export. They earn some money, but now must buy food from someone else. When rose prices fall, they can't afford food. Growing your own food gives you security. Depending on a volatile global market for your survival is risky.
