## 🎯 Exam Question This Covers

> *"Rostow Model presented the five classic stages of economic development which every country must pass through. However, the dependency theorists argue that Rostow's model is not only largely 'Eurocentric', it also ignores the historical economic realities of the west. Explain."*

---

## 1. Origin & Core Premise

- Emerged **1950s–60s** as reaction to modernization theory
- Rooted in Marxist analysis of capitalism and imperialism
- **André Gunder Frank (1967):** *"Underdevelopment is not an original state — it's a result of economic capture and control of backward regions by advanced metropolitan capitalism"*

### Centre vs Periphery

| Centre (Metropolis) | Periphery (Satellite) |
|--------------------|----------------------|
| Developed countries | Developing countries |
| Control technology, capital, markets | Provide raw materials, cheap labour |
| Political/military power | Markets for finished goods |

> *The development of the metropolis WAS the underdevelopment of the periphery.*

---

## 2. Dependency Theory's Critique of Rostow (9 Arguments)

| # | Argument |
|---|----------|
| 1 | **Eurocentric** — Western experience is not universal |
| 2 | **Ignores colonial history** — underdevelopment was CREATED, not natural |
| 3 | **Ignores how the West actually developed** — through colonial extraction, not just internal effort |
| 4 | **Assumes equal starting points** — developing countries suffered colonialism, extraction, debt |
| 5 | **Ignores centre-periphery relationship** — global economy KEEPS periphery dependent |
| 6 | **Unequal trade** — raw materials sold cheap, manufactures bought expensive |
| 7 | **Foreign aid conditionalities** — "help" becomes another form of control |
| 8 | **Too linear** — development has ups and downs, not a straight line |
| 9 | **Blames developing countries for their own poverty** — ignores external domination |

## 3. Key Theorists

| Theorist | Key Work | Contribution |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **André Gunder Frank** | *The Development of Underdevelopment* (1967) | Underdevelopment created by capitalism; only revolution could break exploitation |
| **Walter Rodney** | *How Europe Underdeveloped Africa* (1972) | Detailed how colonialism systematically extracted African wealth |
| **Samir Amin** | — | Imperialism = "total domination" of peripheral regions |
| **Immanuel Wallerstein** | World Systems Theory | Global capitalism structurally advantages the core |
| **J. Matunhu** | — | Both MT and DT failed Africa; proposed African Renaissance theory |

---

## 4. Mechanisms of Exploitation

| Mechanism | How It Works |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Unequal exchange** | Cheap raw materials out, expensive manufactures in |
| **Profit repatriation** | Surplus created by periphery labour extracted to centre |
| **Brain drain** | Skilled professionals emigrate to developed world |
| **Cultural destruction** | Missionary education destroyed pre-capitalist structures |
| **Neocolonial control** | Political independence ≠ economic independence |
| **Conditionality** | Aid/loans with policy conditions serving donor interests |

---

## 5. Deteriorating Terms of Trade (Examples)

| Year | Bananas for 1 Tractor | Rubber for Tractors |
|------|----------------------|-------------------|
| 1960 | 3 tons | 25 tons = 6 tractors |
| 1970 | **11 tons** | 25 tons = **2 tractors** |

> *"Total loss of foreign exchange earnings to Africa due to falling prices... exceeded all foreign funds invested, loaned or granted to Africa"* (Lappé 1977)
