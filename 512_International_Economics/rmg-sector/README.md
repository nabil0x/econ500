## 1. The Anomaly: Import Growth > Export Growth

| Metric | FY21 | FY22 |
|--------|------|------|
| RMG raw material import growth | — | **+51.2%** |
| RMG export growth | — | **+24.6%** |

> Import growth was **nearly double** export growth — unprecedented in Bangladesh's RMG history.

---

## 2. Declining Value Addition

| Period | Raw Materials as % of Export Value |
|--------|-----------------------------------|
| FY21 | **69%** |
| FY22 | **83%** |
| Jan-Mar FY22 | **Over 90%** |

- Bangladesh Bank rule: imports should not exceed **75%** of export value
- Industry says **80%** is survival threshold
- **Current trajectory is unsustainable**

## 5. UNCTAD Framework

From UNCTAD Trade and Development Report 2013:

**Core argument:** Developing countries must shift from **export-oriented → domestic-demand-oriented growth** due to prolonged slow growth in developed economies.

**Recommendations:**
1. Stronger public sector role
2. More equal income distribution (minimum wage, welfare)
3. Supportive monetary/credit policies
4. South-South cooperation
5. Better regulation of commodity markets

## 6. Key Implications for Bangladesh

| Issue | Implication |
|-------|-------------|
| Value addition eroding | Becoming cutting-and-trading, not manufacturing |
| Thin margins | Exporter survival threatened |
| Possible over-invoicing | Capital flight risk |
| Raw material price vulnerability | Global shocks directly impact profitability |
| LDC graduation (2026) | Loss of GSP preferences |
