3rd Tutorial Questions

1. What led to the development of Modernization theory back in the 1940s and 50s? Describe at least 4 critiques of Modernization theory.

2. The World Bank has been prescribing various policy conditionality to developing countries attached to its loans. Discuss the underlying motive as well as the impact of the following three policies commonly prescribed by the World Bank:
   a. Fiscal Discipline
   b. Removal of trade barriers
   c. Privatization of state-owned industries

3. What led to the development of Modernization theory back in the 1940s? Discuss four critiques of Modernization theory with examples.

4. Rostow Model presented the five classic stages of economic development which every country must pass through. However, the dependency theorists argue that Rostow’s model is not only largely ‘Eurocentric’, it also ignores the historical economic realities of the west. Explain.

5. a. Describe at least three economic policies which are prescribed by the Washington consensus under the direction of the World Bank and IMF. Discuss why such policies are prescribed by the World Bank. Discuss the impact of such policies on the developing countries.
   b. Recommend at least three trade/economic policies which might help a particular local industry in Bangladesh to flourish in the face of global competition (Use examples from class discussion).
