## 1. Core Thesis

The global food crisis is NOT caused by population growth or rising demand in China/India. It's caused by **three structural contradictions**:

1. **Food for humans vs feed for animals**
2. **Food for domestic consumption vs food for export**
3. **Food vs fuel (biofuels)**

---

## 2. The Food-Feed Competition

### Inefficiency of Meat Production

| Product | Calories (direct) | Grain Input | Calories (grain) | Loss |
|---------|------------------|-------------|------------------|------|
| 1 kg beef | 1,140 cal | 7+ kg grain | **24,150 cal** | ~95% |
| 1 kg chicken | — | 2 kg grain | — | Significant |
| 1 litre milk | — | 0.2 kg grain | — | Moderate |

### Grain Conversion Ratios
| Product | Grain Required |
|---------|---------------|
| 1 litre milk | 0.2 kg grain |
| 1 kg eggs/chicken | 2 kg grain |
| 1 kg beef | **≥7 kg grain** |

### Rich vs Poor Consumption
| Country Group | Grain/Person/Year | Indirect Share |
|--------------|------------------|----------------|
| **U.S.** | ~900 kg | **80%** (animal feed) |
| **Poor countries** | ~130 kg | Minimal |

> Rich countries consume **7× more grain per person**

## 3. The Export Problem

- IMF, World Bank, WTO pushed export specialization
- Countries told to import food, export flowers/vegetables/oils
- Grain procurement and public distribution systems dismantled
- Land diverted from food crops → export crops, animal feed, biofuels

### Colonial Continuity
| Colonial Era | Modern Era |
|-------------|------------|
| India starved while exporting wheat | India eats less while exporting flowers |
| Colonies forced to export sugar, rice, cotton | Countries told to specialize in exports |

---

## 4. India's Paradox

| Indicator | Data |
|-----------|------|
| GDP growth | ~8% |
| Per capita income growth | ~6% |
| 10% income rise → meat demand | **14–16% rise** |
| Animal product demand growth | **9% annually** |
| Rural population: ↓ calorie & protein | **80% of rural population** |

### Shocking Decline
- Average Indian family (2005): **110 kg less grain/year** than 1991
- NSS data: Calories ↓ for 80% of rural population

---

## 5. Poverty Line Manipulation

| Country | Official Poverty Line | Reality |
|---------|----------------------|---------|
| **India** (2005) | ₹356/month (~₹12/day) | Not enough for 1 kg rice |
| **China** (2007) | ¥2.92/day | Rice costs ¥4/kg |

> These are **destitution lines**, not poverty lines. Lowering standards makes poverty "look" reduced.

---

## 6. Biofuels Crisis

| Metric | 2002 | 2008 |
|--------|------|------|
| U.S. corn for ethanol | 27M tons | **>100M tons** |
| Share of U.S. maize for fuel | — | **~⅓** |

Effect: Global food stocks disappeared → prices skyrocketed → developing countries can't rely on imports

---

## 7. Key Statistics to Remember

- World cereal output per capita: 335 kg (1980s) → **310 kg** (2000s)
- U.S. grain consumption: **900 kg/person/year** vs poor countries: **130 kg**
- 1 kg beef = 7+ kg grain = 95% calorie loss
- Indian family consumed **110 kg less grain/year** in 2005 than 1991
- India's poverty line: **₹12/day** — not enough for 1 kg rice
- U.S. corn for ethanol: 27M → **100M+ tons** (2002–08)
- **⅓ of U.S. maize** used for fuel
