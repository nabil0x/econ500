## 1. Origins (1940s–50s)

| Factor | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Post-WWII context** | Western countries wanted to spread scientific/industrial progress; colonialism no longer acceptable |
| **Cold War geopolitics** | Needed to align developing nations with capitalism vs Soviet communism |
| **Truman 1949** | "Bold new programme for making benefits of scientific advances available for underdeveloped areas" |
| **Marshall Plan model** | Success of European reconstruction became template |
| **Eurocentrism** | Western = advanced; developing = backward, needing guidance |

**Core idea:** Poor countries are poor because they are **traditional and backward**. They must follow the same path Western countries took.

---

## 2. Rostow's 5 Stages of Economic Growth (1960)

| Stage | Name | Key Characteristics |
|-------|------|---------------------|
| **1** | Traditional Society | Subsistence agriculture, barter, low tech, kinship ties |
| **2** | Pre-conditions for Take-off | Specialization, surplus, trade, savings/investment |
| **3** | Take-off | Industrialization, manufacturing, self-sustaining growth |
| **4** | Drive to Maturity | Diversification, complex technology, less import dependence |
| **5** | Age of Mass Consumption | Mass production, services dominate, high living standards |

**Rostow's key assumption:** Linear, universal path — every country passes through these stages.

---

## 3. Seven Critiques

| # | Critique | Key Arguments |
|---|----------|---------------|
| **1** | **Eurocentrism** | Assumes Western culture/values are superior; treats developing countries' history/knowledge as inferior. Africa had powerful empires (Mali, Songhay) before colonialism |
| **2** | **Oversimplified linear thinking** | Development is not a straight line — war, crisis, disasters can push countries backward |
| **3** | **Top-down / Ignores the poor** | Development imposed by elites/foreign experts; the poor are passive receivers, not participants |
| **4** | **Destroys local culture** | Demands abandoning traditional values → "deculturalization" (Matunhu) |
| **5** | **Creates dependency** | Assumes developing countries cannot develop alone; aid/loans come with conditionalities |
| **6** | **Ignores colonial history** | Underdevelopment was CREATED by colonialism, not natural. *"The development of the West and the underdevelopment of developing countries are connected"* |
| **7** | **Ignores how the West actually developed** | West developed through EXTRACTION from colonies, not just internal effort |

## 4. Key Theorists

| Theorist | Work | Argument |
|----------|------|----------|
| **W.W. Rostow** | *Stages of Economic Growth: A Non-Communist Manifesto* (1960) | 5-stage linear model |
| **Harry Truman** | 1949 inaugural address | Language of "development" replaced old imperialism |
