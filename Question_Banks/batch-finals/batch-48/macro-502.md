### Question 1 — Solow Model [4+3+3+4]
Solow: Y, K, L, A. Growth rate of L = n, A = g, depreciation δ.

a) Briefly discuss assumptions about input, output and production function. What is the production function of capital-augmenting and Hicks-neutral technological progress? (4)

b) Briefly describe the dynamics of k (capital). Why is it called the Balanced Growth Path (BGP)? (3)

c) Describe how each affects break-even and actual investment:
   i) Increase in depreciation rate.
   ii) Improvement in technological progress.
   iii) Capital's share α rises. (3)

d) Show impacts of savings ratio in steady state. Prove exogenous changes in savings ratio leave only level effects on k and y, not growth effects. (4)

c) Calculate its effect on the steady state level of technological progress. (3)

### Question 3 — Ramsey-Cass-Koopmans [4+4+2+4]
Y = F(K, AL), utility: U = ∫e^{-ρt} u(c(t)) L(t)/H dt. CRRA: u(c) = (c^{1-θ} - 1)/(1-θ).

a) Define Euler equation. Find the Euler equation for the household's maximization problem and interpret. (4)
b) Describe the phase diagram in terms of evolution of c and k. (4)
c) What is the difference between BGP of Solow and RCK on social optimum and Golden Rule? (2)
d) Determine slope of saddle path using Taylor approximation and rate of adjustment. (4)

### Question 4 — Overlapping Generations [4+3+4+5]
Y_t = F(K_t, A_tL_t), CRS, Inada conditions. A_{t+1} = (1+g)A_t, L_{t+1} = (1+n)L_t. Discrete time, two-period lives.

a) Solve individual's maximization problem where θ determines response to r vs ρ differences. (4)
b) Find the speed of convergence of k to k*. How does it differ from Solow? (3)
c) Describe how each affects k_{t+1} as a function of k_t: (i) Rise in n, (ii) Fall in ρ. (4)
d) After relaxing log utility and Cobb-Douglas assumptions, what possibilities exist for k_{t+1} vs k_t? Is there possibility of dynamic inefficiency? (5)

### Question 5 — R&D Growth (Romer/GH/AGH) [2+6+4+4]
R&D model by P. Romer (1990), Grossman & Helpman (1991), Aghion & Howitt (1992).

a) Critically explain role of a_L (R&D labor) for knowledge accumulation dynamics without capital. Is there long-run growth effect from changing a_L? (2+4)

b) Assume θ + β < 1 and n > 0, on BGP. How do the following affect ġ_A = 0 and ġ_K = 0 lines in (g_A, g_K) space:
   i) Decrease in n.
   ii) Increase in a_K. (6)

c) Two cases: β+θ < 1, or β+θ = 1 and n = 0. Difference in dynamics of growth rates between these cases? (4)

d) Briefly explain the nature of Knowledge and determinants of R&D resource allocation, including Learning by Doing. (4)

### Question 8 — New Keynesian DSGE [4+3+4+3]
Q_i = L_i. P perfect information. z_i, m ~ normal. For consumer: Ċ/C = σ(r_t - ρ). Wicksellian: r^n = ρ + σ^{-1}g. Yun (1996): optimal price equation. k = κπ.

a) Explain role of rational expectations in macro policy effectiveness under imperfect information using Lucas' supply curve. (4)
b) Define New Keynesian DSGE model. Determine NK IS equation for continuous time. (3)
c) Determine NK PC curve using Leibniz's rule. Explain "divine coincidence" (Blanchard & Gali). (4)
d) Describe Taylor rule where x (output) and π (inflation) are both jump variables. (3)
