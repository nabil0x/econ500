## Quick Exam Tips

1. **Always name theorists** — Rostow, Frank, Rodney, Kabeer, Sen, Solow, Hicks, Walras — examiners want to see you know WHO said WHAT
2. **Link theorists across topics** — e.g., Frank (dependency) critiques Rostow (modernization); Babb (WC) links to Frank (dependency); Solow (macro) connects to Barro (convergence) which connects to Frank (dependency divergence)
3. **Use phrases like** *"As Naila Kabeer argues..."* or *"Dependency theorists such as Frank and Rodney contend that..."* or *"The Solow model predicts..."*
4. **Micro-to-Macro bridge:** Arrow-Debreu (micro GE existence) → Solow (macro equilibrium), Pareto optimality (micro) → welfare analysis in all courses
5. **For mixed questions** (e.g., Bangladesh convergence): use Solow (macro) + Kabeer (development) + Frank (political economy) for a three-course answer
