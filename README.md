## 🏥 ECON 507 — Health Economics

> **Source:** Batch 46 (Hons 2020), Batch 47 (MSc 2022), Batch 48 (MSc 2023)

| [Basics →](./health-economics/basics.md) | Health care markets & policy | 🎯 Practice: [Batch 46](../batch-finals/batch-46/health-404.md) · [47](../batch-finals/batch-47/health-507.md) · [48](../batch-finals/batch-48/health-507.md) |
| # | Topic | Full Notes |
|---|-------|------------|
| 1 | All Questions | [README](./health-economics/README.md) |

**Topics Covered (from batch finals):**
- **Micro foundations:** Health care as distinct good, supply/demand tools, asymmetric information, Lemons Principle, adverse selection (pooling vs separating equilibrium)
- **Insurance:** Tax system and demand, labor supply effects, ACA dependent coverage, perfect insurance market
- **Valuation methods:** CDA with Bayesian theorem, TTO, SG, Rating Scale, CEA, CBA, CUA, QALYs, ICER, value of life
- **Policy applications:** Long COVID productivity, minimum wage & child health, LAFPP, supported employment, school meals, ED triage
- **Health system challenges:** Cost escalation, SID, "sick" health care system, public instruments
