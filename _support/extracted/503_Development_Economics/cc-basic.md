## Slide 7

Case Study: 1998 Flood

In FY 1999, Revenue fell short of target by 5.2%

Expenditure exceeded target by 5.2%

Exacerbating budget deficit on current account

For the first time in recent years expenditure under ADP exceeded its target by 2.4%

This surge in ADP expenditure in part reflected the demand of post-flood rehab. Program, where expenditure for repairing damage to infrastructure was needed to be stepped up

Overall deficit in public expenditure rose by 52.2 BnTk.

Deficit was financed through a combination of enhanced deficit financing through the banking system and foreign Aid

## Slide 10

Understanding of Resilience

The ability of a substance or object to spring back into shape; elasticity.

The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties;

Robustness with a certain degree of flexibility

Bounce back alone is not desirable (+Thrive)

Definition

Resilience in a socio-economic system is defined as the capacity of a system to absorb disturbance and reorganize while undergoing change so as to still retain essentially the same function, structure, identity, and feedbacks (Holling, 1973; Walker et al., 2004).
