## Quick Summary

### Final Examinations

| Subject | Key Topics |
|---------|------------|
| **Micro 501** | Preference approach, convexity, Cobb-Douglas, production sets, CV/EV/CS, General equilibrium existence/uniqueness/stability, Edgeworth box, contract curve |
| **Macro 502** | Solow model (extensive), Ramsey-Cass-Koopmans, R&D endogenous growth (Romer/GH/AGH), Real Business Cycle, New Keynesian (Lucas supply, NK IS/PC, Taylor rule) |
| **Dev 503** | Neocolonial dependence, Dualistic thesis, False paradigm, Convergence theory, IMF conditionality, Population growth, Gender equity, Feminization of agriculture |
| **Econ 504** | Panel data (FEM, ECM), LPM/Logit/Probit, Distributed lag models, Simultaneous equations & 2SLS/ILS, Stationarity & unit root, Identification & Hausman test, ARIMA/VAR model |
| **Env 506** | Utilitarianism & welfare, EKC hypothesis, Pollution control (Pigouvian tax/permits), CVM & non-market valuation, Climate change adaptation, Environmental Kuznets curve, CBA of environmental projects |
| **Health 507** | Defining HE, Health care features, Public instruments, Clinical Decision Analysis (Bayesian), CUA, TTO/SG, Adverse selection, SID, EQ-5D, CEA, Value of life |
| **Labor 510** | Female labor force participation, Time allocation (work-leisure), On-the-job training (general vs specific), Robot adoption & wage inequality, SAHO, Monopsony & minimum wage, Education & screening |
| **Political 512** | Washington Consensus, Modernization theory, Dependency theory, GATT-WTO, IMF-World Bank, Export-oriented model critique, MNCs, Dairy protectionism, Mercantilism |

### Tutorial Examinations

| Subject | Key Topics |
|---------|------------|
| **Micro 501** | Cobb-Douglas demand & indirect utility, Roy's identity, Expenditure minimization, CV/EV/CS, Production sets, Profit function, Returns to scale, Elasticity of substitution |
| **Macro 502** | Solow saving ratio & golden rule, Ramsey-Cass-Koopmans (Euler equation, phase diagram), OLG (individual maximization, convergence), R&D (Romer/GH/AH, learning by doing), RBC (household problem, labor supply, Hansen indivisible labor) |
| **Econ 504** | Time series (stochastic/stationary process, ARIMA, cointegration, AEG test), Logit/Probit/FEM/LSDV/ECM, Distributed lag & Koyck, Simultaneous equations (identification, ILS, 2SLS, Hausman test, recursive models) |
| **Health 507** | CDA with Bayes theorem (posterior probability, sensitivity analysis), QALY/HRQoL & utility values (RS, TTO, SG), DHIS2 five-phase implementation in Bangladesh |
| **Labor 510** | Labor market forces (supply/demand), Efficient time allocation (work-leisure), Female participation trends, Employee selection issues, General vs specific on-the-job training, University education investment decision rules |
