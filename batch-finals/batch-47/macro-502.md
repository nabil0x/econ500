### Question 1 — Solow Growth Model
Four variables: output (Y), capital (K), labor (L), knowledge/effectiveness of labor (A). Growth rate of L = n, growth rate of A = g, depreciation rate = δ. [4+3+3+4]

a) Briefly discuss the assumptions about input, output and production function to construct the Solow exogenous growth model.

b) Briefly describe the phase diagram for k. Why does it follow the Balanced Growth Path (BGP)?

c) Describe how each of the following affects break-even and actual investment:
   i) The rate of depreciation falls.
   ii) The rate of technological progress rises.
   iii) Capital's share (α) rises.

d) Show the impacts of saving ratio in steady state and prove that exogenous changes in the saving ratio leave only level effects, not growth effects.

### Question 2 — Solow: Golden Rule & Convergence
Solow economy on BGP with Cobb-Douglas Y = F(K, AL). Saving rate s exogenous, depreciation δ. L and A grow at n and g. [3+5+3+3]

a) Show the impacts of changes in saving ratio on per capita consumption. Does any steady state solution automatically imply the Golden Rule? Discuss.

b) Explain the long-run effect of a rise in saving on output. In the vicinity of BGP, how rapidly does the economy converge?

c) Find the elasticity of y* with respect to n. If α(k*) = 1/3, g = 2%, δ = 3%, by how much does a fall in n from 2% to 1% raise y*?

d) Briefly explain growth accounting and convergence as empirical applications.

### Question 4 — Two-Period OLG
Y_t = F(K_t, A_tL_t) with CRS, Inada conditions. A_{t+1} = (1+g)A_t, L_{t+1} = (1+n)L_t. Discrete time, individuals live two periods. [5+2+3+4]

a) Solve the individual's maximization problem where θ determines how consumption varies in response to differences between r and ρ. Explain the role of income effect and substitution effect on individual's consumption and saving.

b) Find an expression for k_{t+1} as a function of k_t.

c) Describe how each affects k_{t+1} as a function of k_t:
   i) A rise in g.
   ii) A fall in ρ.
   iii) A permanent increase in government purchases.

d) Find the speed of convergence of k to k*. How does it differ from Solow?

### Question 5 — R&D Endogenous Growth (Romer/GH/AGH)
Consider a simplified R&D model by P. Romer (1990), Grossman & Helpman (1991), Aghion & Howitt (1992). θ reflects effect of knowledge stock on R&D success, β is a shift parameter. [2+4+4+4]

a) How does the process to construct the framework differ from the Solow model?

b) Critically explain the role of a_L (R&D labor) for the dynamics of knowledge accumulation without capital. Is there a long-run growth effect from changing a_L?

c) Assume θ + β < 1 and n > 0, on BGP. Describe how each affects the ġ_A = 0 and ġ_K = 0 lines in (g_A, g_K) space:
   i) An increase in n.
   ii) An increase in a_K.

d) Two cases: (i) β+θ < 1, or (ii) β+θ = 1 and n = 0.
   i) What is the difference in dynamics of growth rates between these cases?
   ii) Distinguish between semi-endogenous and fully endogenous growth models.

### Question 7 — Real Business Cycle
