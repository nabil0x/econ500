## Key Concepts

| Concept | Definition |
|---------|------------|
| **Health economics** | The study of allocation of scarce resources in health and health care |
| **Grossman model** | Health as both consumption and investment good; individuals choose optimal health stock balancing marginal benefit and marginal cost |
| **Production function of health** | H = f(medical care, lifestyle, education, environment, time) — how inputs produce health output |
| **Supplier Induced Demand (SID)** | Providers exploit information advantage to recommend excessive care for financial gain |
| **Lemons Principle** | In health insurance, asymmetric information drives out good risks, leading to market failure |
| **Adverse selection** | High-risk individuals disproportionately buy insurance, raising premiums and potentially unravelling the pool |
| **CEA** | Cost-Effectiveness Analysis — outcomes in natural units (life years saved, cases detected) |
| **CBA** | Cost-Benefit Analysis — all outcomes monetised; compare net social benefits |
| **CUA** | Cost-Utility Analysis — outcomes in QALYs; compare cost per QALY gained |
| **Clinical Decision Analysis (CDA)** | Decision trees under uncertainty; combines prior probability, test characteristics, and utility values |
| **Bayesian theorem** | P(D|T) = P(T|D) × P(D) / P(T) — updates disease probability given test result |
| **TTO** | Time Trade-Off — utility = x/t where x is years in perfect health the patient would accept instead of t years in current state |
| **SG** | Standard Gamble — utility = 1 − p where p is the mortality risk the patient would accept for perfect health |
| **EQ-5D** | EuroQol 5-Dimension — standardised health status measure (mobility, self-care, usual activities, pain/discomfort, anxiety/depression) |
| **Value of life** | Willingness to pay for mortality risk reduction; used in CBA of health interventions |
| **Pooling equilibrium** | All insurance buyers pay same premium regardless of risk type — unsustainable under adverse selection |
| **Separating equilibrium** | Different risk types choose different contracts revealing their type — only possible when low-risk proportion is small |
| **Triage** | Priority-based sorting of emergency patients by medical need |
| **Coinsurance** | Patient's out-of-pocket share of health care costs; reduces moral hazard but may deter necessary care |
| **Optimal health stock** | Level of health capital where marginal efficiency of health investment equals its cost (Grossman) |
